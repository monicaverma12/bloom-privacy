Bloom Watch Face — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Bloom is a watch face for Wear OS smartwatches, published on Google Play by Monica Verma ("I", "me"). This policy explains how Bloom handles your information.

Summary

Bloom does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data.

Information shown on the watch face

Bloom displays the time, date, and your daily step count. The step count is provided by your watch's own system and is shown only on your watch's screen. Bloom does not save it, send it anywhere, or share it with anyone, including me.

No internet access, analytics, or ads

Bloom does not connect to the internet, contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services. It has no account or sign-in.

Purchases

If you buy Bloom, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. I do not receive your payment details. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Bloom does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
